Activity 5, Task B: Training Divides 1

 

 

 [KK1]The rational numbers greater than 1 are all b/a where a < b (and a is not 0). This follows from the definition of rational numbers as the ratio of two integers and that for division to produce a quotient greater than 1 the divisor must be less than the dividend.

 

 [KK2]In the next activity we enumerate all the rational numbers. So there is a one-to-one correspondence between the set of rational numbers and its subset, the rational numbers between 0 and 1. Sally’s claim is based upon giving priority to intuitions about sets and subsets over the existence of a one-to-one mapping.
